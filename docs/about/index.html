Pacific Dental Alliance

The dental network families trust.

Affiliated offices delivering quality care, every day.

At Pacific Dental Alliance (PDA), we know that quality dental care comes first. Families and individuals want the best, which is exactly what you get at our network. Dentists in our affiliated offices are experienced and highly reputable. We have some two-dozen affiliated practices conveniently located throughout the Pacific Northwest and Alaska. Our goal? To bring families quality, affordable dental care they can trust.

An unbeatable value for your family.

What good is a network with excellent doctors if families can't afford it? To ensure that everyone has access to quality dental care, we have made it affordable, offering competitive pricing that won't break your bank account. By selecting the finest dental practices in the region and making them affordable for the average family, PDA has solved the current healthcare dilemma: that premium care is too expensive. We are proof that it is not.

A greater range of dental services.

In addition to offering premium dental care with affordable costs, the PDA network of affiliated offices offers convenience as well. The practices in our network deliver a range of in-house services so that families can often visit a single location for all of their dental needs. Our dentists can do it all: implants, molar root canals, oral surgery, and more. Of course, they also provide regular check-ups and cleanings to keep your family’s teeth healthy and strong. Think about it: premium care, affordable cost, at one convenient location. That's what a PDA affiliate offers.
