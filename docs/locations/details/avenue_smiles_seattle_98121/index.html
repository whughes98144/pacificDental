About Avenue Smiles

At Avenue Smiles, Dr. Aditi Agarwal and our Board Certified Dentists and team members are committed to the best safety, comfort, and results for our patients. We utilize state-of-the-art technology and advanced dental services to leave your smile better than ever. Each time you leave our office, you’ll feel good about the improvements you see in your smile.

Located in downtown Seattle, Avenue Smiles provides a full range of general, cosmetic, and emergency dentistry services to those in need of convenient dental care in the metro area. 

About the Doctor

Aditi Agarwal, DMD

When Dr. Aditi Agarwal moved to the United States, she had already attained her dental degree and practiced general dentistry in India. In order to master the science of dentistry as taught in the United States, she went back to school and she earned her DMD from Boston University in 2003. Since then, Dr. Aditi Agarwal has been part of a large group practice in Washington where she has earned the respect of her peers and the trust of her patients.

Priya Bhavan, DDS

Samrudhi Nakhwa, DDS

Dr. Samrudhi Nakhwa received her Bachelors in Dental Surgery degree in India after which she practiced in Mumbai for two years. She graduated from New York University with a Doctorate in Dental Surgery (DDS) in 2016 and has been practicing dentistry in Washington since then. She loves working with pediatric patients and is licensed to practice sedation dentistry on kids and adults. Along with most dental procedures, she is well trained in Invisalign, esthetic dentistry, and other dental surgical procedures. In her free time she likes going trekking and camping with husband and friends. She is also fond of sketching and photography.

Services Offered

Preventative Dentistry

Preventative dentistry is our proactive approach to your dental health. A few simple home care practices, paired with regular visits to your dental professional, go a long way toward maintaining optimal oral health.

Teeth Whitening

We offer three different comprehensive whitening systems to meet your desire for a brighter smile. From in-office whitening to two types of home whitening, we have you covered.

Porcelain Veneers

Porcelain veneers are a quick cosmetic dental fix for chipped, stained, or misaligned teeth. Veneers allow for correction in the shape, size, spacing, and color of your natural teeth.

Tooth-Colored Fillings

Tooth-colored fillings replace silver amalgam fillings with tooth-colored composite resin material and restore the natural color and appearance of your teeth.

Implants

Implants are the best way to replace missing teeth, and are titanium posts that are surgically placed in the bone, which then have crowns placed on them once the bone has healed around the implant.

Root Canal Therapy

At some point, most people will undergo a root canal. Our goal always is to save your natural teeth and prevent the need for implants, bridges, and partial dentures.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Your complete oral health is our goal, and that includes a positive self-image that comes when you feel good about your teeth, mouth, and smile. Whether it’s subtle enhancements or dramatic natural, esthetic makeovers, we can help.

Sedation

We have a special certification to provide dental oral conscious sedation that allows patients to be completely at ease under sedation at your dental appointment. From a quick pill to more extensive sedation, we offer multiple choices to keep you happy and calm.
