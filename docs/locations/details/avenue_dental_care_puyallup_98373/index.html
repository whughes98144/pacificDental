About Avenue Dental Care

Over the years, our team of experienced dentists have generated lots of satisfied and happy patients because of what we have to offer. Our clinic is known for being able to provide a friendly atmosphere to our patients, where we make them exceptionally comfortable. Our dentists are highly reputed and can conduct treatments and dental solutions for all kinds of dental issues and concerns. We are equipped with the latest technology and modern methodologies, making us an excellent provider for the whole family.

About the Doctor

Varun Sharma, DMD

Dr. Sharma graduated from the Boston University Dental School in 2002 and moved to the Pacific Northwest the same year. After falling in love with the area, he bought the practice, making Washington his permanent home. In 2010, after two years of extensive training in cosmetic and neuromuscular dentistry, Dr. Sharma completed his studies offered by the Las Vegas Institute of Cosmetic Dentistry. He also became licensed to perform oral and IV sedation.

He now lives in the Seattle area with his wife and two daughters. In his free time, he enjoys exploring the many outdoor activities that the Pacific Northwest area has to offer.

Services Offered

Preventative Dentistry

Preventative dentistry is our proactive approach to your dental health. A few simple home care practices, paired with regular visits to your dental professional, go a long way toward maintaining optimal oral health.

Teeth Whitening

We offer three different comprehensive whitening systems to meet your desire for a brighter smile. From in-office whitening to two types of home whitening, we have you covered.

Porcelain Veneers

Porcelain veneers are a quick cosmetic dental fix for chipped, stained, or misaligned teeth. Veneers allow for correction in the shape, size, spacing, and color of your natural teeth.

Tooth-Colored Fillings

Tooth-colored fillings replace silver amalgam fillings with tooth-colored composite resin material and restore the natural color and appearance of your teeth.

Implants

Implants are the best way to replace missing teeth, and are titanium posts that are surgically placed in the bone, which then have crowns placed on them once the bone has healed around the implant.

Oral Surgery

No patient looks forward to oral surgery. We understand. Our high-tech procedures and expert dental team ensure your comfort—whether it’s the extraction of wisdom teeth or a decayed or broken teeth.

Root Canal Therapy

At some point, most people will undergo a root canal. Our goal always is to save your natural teeth and prevent the need for implants, bridges, and partial dentures.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Your complete oral health is our goal, and that includes a positive self-image that comes when you feel good about your teeth, mouth, and smile. Whether it’s subtle enhancements or dramatic natural, esthetic makeovers, we can help.

Interdisciplinary Dentistry

We work closely with a team of talented specialists, including orthodontists, oral surgeons, periodontists, and endodontists to help bring you the best treatment options.
