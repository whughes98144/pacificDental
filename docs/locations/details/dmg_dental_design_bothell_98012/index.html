About the Doctor

David Myaskovsky DDS, MSD

At DMG Dental, we provide highly personalized, comprehensive dental care in the most advanced dental office in the Northwest.

With over 35 years of clinical experience and advanced technology combined with innovative techniques bring best results to your oral health. our doctors strive to ensure that our patients receive the highest level of quality and care. But most importantly. honesty and compassion create a solid foundation for a healthy partnership with you.

Our expertise ranges from routine diagnostic treatment and consistant preventative care to a new smile we'll create a customised plan that feels right for you. From Invisalign or Crear Aligners to veneers, from comfortable root canals, performed under the microscope to cosmetic dentistry procedures such as porcelain veneers, implants, full mouth reconstruction and zoom whitening, 3D technology...discover how the advanced dentistry enhanced your life.

The core philosophy at DMG Dental is to provide exceptional dental treatment in a safe and comfortable environment. That is why we have invested in digital X-ray systems that expose patients to 90 percent less radiation. Our laser cavity detectors and curing systems allow us to provide faster, painless, and more precise treatments.

By investing in the latest technology available to modern dentistry we are also able to better diagnose and treat dental problems. We use intra-oral magnification and computer imaging systems that spot and capture the smallest details. We also use the most advanced laser technology available so that treatment is as precise and painless as possible.

Our doctors and staff make going to the dentist an enjoyable experience. During your visit to DMG Dental you can watch TV or a DVD, or listen to music.

Services Offered

Preventative Dentistry

Preventative dentistry is our proactive approach to your dental health. A few simple home care practices, paired with regular visits to your dental professional, go a long way toward maintaining optimal oral health.

Teeth Whitening

We offer three different comprehensive whitening systems to meet your desire for a brighter smile. From in-office whitening to two types of home whitening, we have you covered.

Porcelain Veneers

Porcelain veneers are a quick cosmetic dental fix for chipped, stained, or misaligned teeth. Veneers allow for correction in the shape, size, spacing, and color of your natural teeth.

Tooth-Colored Fillings

Tooth-colored fillings replace silver amalgam fillings with tooth-colored composite resin material and restore the natural color and appearance of your teeth.

Same-Day Crowns

Natural looking same-day crowns are made in two hours, which means no messy impressions or temporary crowns.

Implants

Implants are the best way to replace missing teeth, and are titanium posts that are surgically placed in the bone, which then have crowns placed on them once the bone has healed around the implant.

Oral Surgery

No patient looks forward to oral surgery. We understand. Our high-tech procedures and expert dental team ensure your comfort—whether it’s the extraction of wisdom teeth or a decayed or broken teeth.

Root Canal Therapy

At some point, most people will undergo a root canal. Our goal always is to save your natural teeth and prevent the need for implants, bridges, and partial dentures.

3D Dentistry

By using 3D technology to create digital impressions, uncomfortable, traditional impression putty is no longer needed, plus impression times are drastically reduced.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Your complete oral health is our goal, and that includes a positive self-image that comes when you feel good about your teeth, mouth, and smile. Whether it’s subtle enhancements or dramatic natural, esthetic makeovers, we can help.

Interdisciplinary Dentistry

We work closely with a team of talented specialists, including orthodontists, oral surgeons, periodontists, and endodontists to help bring you the best treatment options.

Sedation

We have a special certification to provide dental oral conscious sedation that allows patients to be completely at ease under sedation at your dental appointment. From a quick pill to more extensive sedation, we offer multiple choices to keep you happy and calm.
