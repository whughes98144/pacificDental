About Alderwood Dentistry

At Alderwood Dentistry we are committed to building long-lasting relationships by delivering high-quality family dental care in a warm, compassionate environment. We believe that every patient’s dental situation is unique. Therefore we personalize your treatment as we listen to your needs and dental health goals. We offer a wide range of services, including specialty services, while utilizing the latest technology to create a seamless and prompt experience. Patients truly appreciate our transparency and communication, as we help to navigate dental options while keeping a wide range of budgets and insurances in mind. Not only will you leave our office with a healthier, more beautiful smile, but also with a positive dental experience that will make coming to the dentist enjoyable!

About the Doctor

Dr. Regina Lam

Dr. Regina Lam was born and raised in Hawaii. She did her undergraduate studies at the University of Pennsylvania in Philadelphia and moved to Seattle over 12 years ago to attend the University of Washington for dental school where she obtained her dental degree. GO DAWGS!

Dr. Lam has wanted to be a dentist since the second grade, being inspired by having a great childhood dentist herself. She loves providing quality, comprehensive care for the whole family and strives to create positive dental experiences with her aloha spirit. Dr. Lam is committed to always further advancing her dental knowledge and keeping up-to-date with the latest technology and materials so that she can provide her patients with the highest quality of care. She was the youngest dentist to ever to be selected to serve on the Washington State Dental Association’s Pacific Northwest Dental Conference committee, where she helped organize and select speakers for the state’s largest continuing education conference for the entire dental community. Having practiced in Lynnwood for the past six years, Dr. Lam has truly enjoyed getting to know all of her patients and serving the communities of Lynnwood, Edmonds, Bothell, and Mill Creek as their trusted, local dental provider. 

In her free time, Dr. Lam and her husband take advantage of the great Pacific Northwest, skiing and soaking up as much outdoor time as possible with lots of walks to neighborhood parks with their sons.

Dr. Stephanie Tran

Dr. Stephanie Tran was born and raised in Bellevue, Washington, and is excited to be giving back to the community that helped shape her. 

Dr. Tran attended the University of Washington where she earned a bachelor's degree in biochemistry with a minor in music. She then continued her studies at the University of Washington School of Dentistry, earning a doctorate of dental surgery. After graduation, she completed an Advanced Education in General Dentistry residency through New York University Langone, a valuable experience that allowed her to expand her dental knowledge while aiding underserved populations in south King County. 

In her practice, Dr. Tran believes every patient should be empowered with the knowledge to be actively involved in their individual treatment plan. She hopes to inspire all of her patients towards a healthier life. 

Dr. Tran is a member of the Seattle King County Dental Society, the Washington State Dental Association, and the American Dental Association. 

In her free time, Dr. Tran likes to keep active by running, hiking, and snowboarding. She also enjoys experiencing new foods through cooking and traveling.

Services Offered

Preventative Dentistry

Preventative dentistry is our proactive approach to your dental health. A few simple home care practices, paired with regular visits to your dental professional, go a long way toward maintaining optimal oral health.

Teeth Whitening

We offer three different comprehensive whitening systems to meet your desire for a brighter smile. From in-office whitening to two types of home whitening, we have you covered.

Porcelain Veneers

Porcelain veneers are a quick cosmetic dental fix for chipped, stained, or misaligned teeth. Veneers allow for correction in the shape, size, spacing, and color of your natural teeth.

Tooth-Colored Fillings

Tooth-colored fillings replace silver amalgam fillings with tooth-colored composite resin material and restore the natural color and appearance of your teeth.

Implants

Implants are the best way to replace missing teeth, and are titanium posts that are surgically placed in the bone, which then have crowns placed on them once the bone has healed around the implant.

Oral Surgery

No patient looks forward to oral surgery. We understand. Our high-tech procedures and expert dental team ensure your comfort—whether it’s the extraction of wisdom teeth or a decayed or broken teeth.

Root Canal Therapy

At some point, most people will undergo a root canal. Our goal always is to save your natural teeth and prevent the need for implants, bridges, and partial dentures.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Your complete oral health is our goal, and that includes a positive self-image that comes when you feel good about your teeth, mouth, and smile. Whether it’s subtle enhancements or dramatic natural, esthetic makeovers, we can help.

Sedation

We have a special certification to provide dental oral conscious sedation that allows patients to be completely at ease under sedation at your dental appointment. From a quick pill to more extensive sedation, we offer multiple choices to keep you happy and calm.
