About the Doctor

Donghyun Koo, DDS

In 2003, Dr. Koo moved to Mclean, Virginia to attend the University of Virginia where he graduated with a Bachelor of Science in Mathematics. Pursuant of higher education, Dr. Koo continued his studies at New York University College of Dentistry to receive his Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS).

Dr. Koo is committed to pursue advanced continuing education and has years of cooperative dental work with specialists that include periodontists, endodontists, and oral surgeons. He is experienced in various aspects of restorative dentistry, surgical extractions, complex dental caries, crowns and bridges, dental implants, removable prosthodontics, endodontic therapy, and periodontal treatment.

With a discerning eye for detail and ambition for patient satisfaction, he founded Best Dentistry in Crown Hill, Seattle in 2020. He and all his staff will treat each and every patient like his own family.

Nick Yeung, DDS

With a soft touch, engaging personality and attention to detail, Dr. Nick Yeung prides himself in establishing a natural rapport with his patients. He truly believes in patient-centered care in which he will work with you to choose the treatment that best suits your needs.

Dr. Yeung grew up in the Pacific Northwest and attended the University of Washington to obtain his undergraduate degree in Economics. He furthered his education at the University of Washington School of Dentistry to receive his Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree in 2015. His enthusiastic passion never ceases, so he actively participates in hundreds of hours of continuing education every year to stay up-to-date with the latest technology and techniques in dentistry.

He is proficient in all aspects of dental care including restorative, cosmetic, surgical extractions, root canal treatment and dental implants. He is also a graduate of the prestigious Kois Center in Seattle where he is trained in comprehensive dental care.

Avery Ruben, DMD

Dr. Avery Ruben completed his Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) degree at Nova Southeastern University in 2016, where he graduated with the prestigious honor of Omicron Kappa Upsilon. This recognition highlights his exceptional clinical skills and his commitment to ethical and professional standards.

Prior to his dental studies, Dr. Ruben earned his undergraduate degree from the University of Florida, in food science and human nutrition.

With a passion for staying at the forefront of dental advancements, Dr. Ruben regularly engages in continuing education and professional development opportunities. He utilizes the latest techniques and state-of-the-art technology to deliver personalized, comprehensive dental care to his patients including dental implants and clear aligners.

Services Offered

Preventative Dentistry

Preventative dentistry is our proactive approach to your dental health. A few simple home care practices, paired with regular visits to your dental professional, go a long way toward maintaining optimal oral health.

Teeth Whitening

We offer three different comprehensive whitening systems to meet your desire for a brighter smile. From in-office whitening to two types of home whitening, we have you covered.

Porcelain Veneers

Porcelain veneers are a quick cosmetic dental fix for chipped, stained, or misaligned teeth. Veneers allow for correction in the shape, size, spacing, and color of your natural teeth.

Tooth-Colored Fillings

Tooth-colored fillings replace silver amalgam fillings with tooth-colored composite resin material and restore the natural color and appearance of your teeth.

Implants

Implants are the best way to replace missing teeth, and are titanium posts that are surgically placed in the bone, which then have crowns placed on them once the bone has healed around the implant.

Oral Surgery

No patient looks forward to oral surgery. We understand. Our high-tech procedures and expert dental team ensure your comfort—whether it’s the extraction of wisdom teeth or a decayed or broken teeth.

Root Canal Therapy

At some point, most people will undergo a root canal. Our goal always is to save your natural teeth and prevent the need for implants, bridges, and partial dentures.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Your complete oral health is our goal, and that includes a positive self-image that comes when you feel good about your teeth, mouth, and smile. Whether it’s subtle enhancements or dramatic natural, esthetic makeovers, we can help.
