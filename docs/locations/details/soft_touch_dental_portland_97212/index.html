About Soft Touch Dental

Trust Soft Touch Dental for all your dental needs
Professional, gentle and kind — Soft Touch Dental is your one-stop location for taking care of all your family’s dental needs.

From regular cleanings, checkups and cosmetic treatments, to advanced dental surgery, our doctors put your comfort first.

Your comfort and health are our top priorities.

Nervous? Don’t be. We specialize in treating patients who are a little “shy” about seeing the dentist. Not only are we friendly, we help you relax with a local anesthetic, or you can enjoy a short snooze while getting your teeth sparkling clean.

Soft Touch Dental is convenient: We have five offices to serve you in Portland, Gresham, Springfield, Tualatin and Woodburn.

Call us today and schedule your appointment for a brighter smile.

About the Doctor

Dr. Danny Sadakah

Dr. Sadakah believes that patients deserve all available treatment options at an affordable cost. At Soft Touch Dental, he is committed to providing a one-stop practice where all of a patient’s dental needs can be fulfilled.

Dr Sadakah graduated from Portland State University in 2000. He received his Doctorate at Boston University, where he studied in the oral Surgery Department. He graduated at the top of his class in 2004, receiving many awards.

A very family-oriented person, Dr. Sadakah enjoys spending time with his wife and two children. He loves the cool and rain in the Pacific Northwest, and also is an avid sports fan.

Dr. Sarah Mazareei

Dr. Mazareei’s practice philosophy is treating each patient as though she were treating a family member. She loves her profession, and is passionate about helping patients achieve good oral health and beautiful smiles. Dr. Mazareei is gentle during procedures to make every patient’s visit as pleasant as possible. Her aim is to provide them with the best possible esthetic and functional outcome.

She obtained her Bachelors of Science degree in Biomedical Sciences from the University of South Florida, graduating Summa Cum Laude. She is a graduate of Oregon Health and Sciences University where she earned her Doctorate of Dental Medicine.
She enjoys spending time with family and friends, reading, hiking and traveling.

Dr. Robert Wilde

According to Dr. Wilde, good dental health begins with daily dental hygiene, exposure to fluoride and regular checkups.

Dr. Wilde graduated from Lewis and Clark College in Portland with a Bachelors degree in chemistry and psychology. He is a graduate of Oregon Health and Science University, where he earned his Doctorate of Dental Medicine.
Dr. Wilde enjoys time with his family and friends. In his spare time, he jogs, bikes and watches his kids play soccer.

Services Offered

Preventative Dentistry

Preventative dentistry is our proactive approach to your dental health. A few simple home care practices, paired with regular visits to your dental professional, go a long way toward maintaining optimal oral health.

Teeth Whitening

We offer three different comprehensive whitening systems to meet your desire for a brighter smile. From in-office whitening to two types of home whitening, we have you covered.

Tooth-Colored Fillings

Tooth-colored fillings replace silver amalgam fillings with tooth-colored composite resin material and restore the natural color and appearance of your teeth.

Implants

Implants are the best way to replace missing teeth, and are titanium posts that are surgically placed in the bone, which then have crowns placed on them once the bone has healed around the implant.

Oral Surgery

No patient looks forward to oral surgery. We understand. Our high-tech procedures and expert dental team ensure your comfort—whether it’s the extraction of wisdom teeth or a decayed or broken teeth.

Root Canal Therapy

At some point, most people will undergo a root canal. Our goal always is to save your natural teeth and prevent the need for implants, bridges, and partial dentures.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Your complete oral health is our goal, and that includes a positive self-image that comes when you feel good about your teeth, mouth, and smile. Whether it’s subtle enhancements or dramatic natural, esthetic makeovers, we can help.

Interdisciplinary Dentistry

We work closely with a team of talented specialists, including orthodontists, oral surgeons, periodontists, and endodontists to help bring you the best treatment options.

Sedation

We have a special certification to provide dental oral conscious sedation that allows patients to be completely at ease under sedation at your dental appointment. From a quick pill to more extensive sedation, we offer multiple choices to keep you happy and calm.
