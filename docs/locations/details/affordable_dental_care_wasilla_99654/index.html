About Affordable Dental Care

Our professional and caring family dentists and staff in Anchorage offer full-service dental care from root canals and gum treatments to tooth-colored fillings, bridges, and implants. Call our office today and ask about our special savings.

About the Doctor

Jason Bressler, DDS

Dr. Bressler grew up in Federal Way, Washington. He played soccer at Seattle University and earned a degree in Biochemistry in 2006. He then continued on to the University of Washington School of Dentistry and graduated in 2010. While at the UW he served as a teaching assistant and was one of the top clinical students.

Dr. Bressler loves all aspects of dentistry and is continually furthering his education in dental implants, sinus lifting, tissue/bone grafting techniques, cosmetic dentistry, and root canal therapy. His goal is to be able to provide the most complete, up-to-date care for his patients so that they don’t have to seek care from another provider.

Dr. Bressler lives in Anchorage with his wife Jana (who is also a dentist) and their two daughters. In his free time he enjoys fishing, snowboarding, hiking, running, playing soccer, and spending time with his family and friends.

Matthew Paige, DDS

Dr. Paige was raised in the Seattle area. He obtained a B.S. in Biochemistry from the University of Washington. During his time there, Dr. Paige volunteered at many dental clinics where he confirmed his interest in the dental field. He decided to continue his education at the University of Washington, where he earned his dental degree.

Dr. Paige enjoys practicing all aspects of dentistry and takes many continuing dental education courses in order to further his knowledge in a wide variety of dental specialties. In his free time, Dr. Paige enjoys spending time with friends and family, traveling, and skiing.

Services Offered

Teeth Whitening

We offer three different comprehensive whitening systems to meet your desire for a brighter smile. From in-office whitening to two types of home whitening, we have you covered.

Porcelain Veneers

Porcelain veneers are a quick cosmetic dental fix for chipped, stained, or misaligned teeth. Veneers allow for correction in the shape, size, spacing, and color of your natural teeth.

Tooth-Colored Fillings

Tooth-colored fillings replace silver amalgam fillings with tooth-colored composite resin material and restore the natural color and appearance of your teeth.

Implants

Implants are the best way to replace missing teeth, and are titanium posts that are surgically placed in the bone, which then have crowns placed on them once the bone has healed around the implant.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Your complete oral health is our goal, and that includes a positive self-image that comes when you feel good about your teeth, mouth, and smile. Whether it’s subtle enhancements or dramatic natural, esthetic makeovers, we can help.
