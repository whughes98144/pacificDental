About Sterling Dental Care

At Sterling Dental Care, our patients are our top priority. When you have a dental problem, you need to turn to a dentist who listens and responds--an experienced doctor who knows the field and can effectively diagnose and treat your needs, a friendly dentist who counsels you on the best ways to maintain and improve your oral health, in addition to improving your smile. Plus, you benefit from a dedicated team of trained professionals who give you the individualized attention you deserve.


The best care in the world doesn't mean anything if you cannot access it. At Sterling Dental Care we strive to make our office friendly and professional. Our dentists, with combined professional experience of over twenty years, and dedicated staff in Federal Way offer full-service dental care from root canals to tooth-colored fillings, bridges, and implants. We service all ages and all types of smiles! Call our office today and ask about our monthly specials.

About the Doctor

Chan Kim, DDS

Dr. Chan Kim grew up in South Korea and attended La Sierra University in California. He then went to Loma Linda University School of Dentistry, where he received his Doctorate of Dental Surgery degree in 2010. 

Dr. Kim strives to create a welcoming environment where your comfort and well-being are at the forefront of his approach. He enjoys all aspects of dentistry and particularly has a keen interest in extractions including wisdom teeth, root canal therapy and implantology. 

When not practicing dentistry, you will find Dr. Kim running in Tiger mountain and listening to piano masterpieces by Chopin and Rachmaninoff.

Services Offered

Preventative Dentistry

Preventative dentistry is our proactive approach to your dental health. A few simple home care practices, paired with regular visits to your dental professional, go a long way toward maintaining optimal oral health.

Teeth Whitening

We offer three different comprehensive whitening systems to meet your desire for a brighter smile. From in-office whitening to two types of home whitening, we have you covered.

Porcelain Veneers

Porcelain veneers are a quick cosmetic dental fix for chipped, stained, or misaligned teeth. Veneers allow for correction in the shape, size, spacing, and color of your natural teeth.

Tooth-Colored Fillings

Tooth-colored fillings replace silver amalgam fillings with tooth-colored composite resin material and restore the natural color and appearance of your teeth.

Implants

Implants are the best way to replace missing teeth, and are titanium posts that are surgically placed in the bone, which then have crowns placed on them once the bone has healed around the implant.

Oral Surgery

No patient looks forward to oral surgery. We understand. Our high-tech procedures and expert dental team ensure your comfort—whether it’s the extraction of wisdom teeth or a decayed or broken teeth.

Root Canal Therapy

At some point, most people will undergo a root canal. Our goal always is to save your natural teeth and prevent the need for implants, bridges, and partial dentures.

3D Dentistry

By using 3D technology to create digital impressions, uncomfortable, traditional impression putty is no longer needed, plus impression times are drastically reduced.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Your complete oral health is our goal, and that includes a positive self-image that comes when you feel good about your teeth, mouth, and smile. Whether it’s subtle enhancements or dramatic natural, esthetic makeovers, we can help.
